Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know a provider is trustworthy?

Every SAMHAL provider is verified and rated by real customers after each job.

What if I'm not satisfied with the service?

Contact our support team within 24 hours and we'll help resolve the issue.

How does payment work?

You can pay via eSewa, Khalti, card, or cash after the service is completed — you're never charged upfront.

Can I reschedule a booking?

Yes, you can reschedule up to 2 hours before your scheduled time through your dashboard.
